# St. John's rework: inventory and plan (2026-11-12)

Investigation only: nothing in the repository was changed. Source: `maps/st_johns.json` (format 36, 3,395 entities),
the owner's brief (`docs/map-briefs/st_johns/OWNER_BRIEF_2026-11-12.md` on origin/main, with "The campus plan" and
"Which building is which"), the twenty references, MapMaker's catalogue as it is in the code, and pictures taken in
MapMaker. Pictures are in `pics/` beside this file (list at the end). Positions are map metres: x east, z north
(north is up, the same way as the school's plan); y up, 0 = 279 m above sea level.

**Measured** means read from the map file or the code. **Inferred** means my reading of photos or the plan; each is marked.

## 0. The short version

1. The map is 2,500 free blocks, 733 props, 73 lights and two set pieces. **It has no tiles, no doors, no glass and no
   interior walls.** Every floor of every hall is one open room with furniture in rows; every window is a bare hole;
   every stair is a stack of 16 stone blocks. That is why it reads as "wide open walls" and "old custom stairs".
2. The layout is right. Block for block it matches the school's plan (same north, same streets, same halls in the
   same places). What is missing is on the plan's edges (Cadet Post, infirmary, store, barns) and inside the halls.
3. MapMaker already has most of the shell: stair tiles (straight and half-turn), the Collegiate stone facade
   (plinth, buttresses, string course, hood moulds, crenellated parapet), window and arch wall styles that take real
   glass, beamed and vaulted ceilings, an iron-bound oak door, the carriageway. It lacks the things that make this
   campus: sash and arched windows, a dorm room, a classroom, round turrets, a cone roof, a clock tower, a wide-span
   arched roof, pews, the cairn, gate piers.
4. Recommendation: **rebuild the halls from tiles and set pieces on MapMaker's 8 m grid**, one hall per landing,
   pieces first. The halls are 16 to 20 m wide, which is two cells: the grid fits them. Each hall moves at most 4 m
   and loses its 0.5 to 2.4 degree turn. This is the largest question for the owner (Q1).

## 0a. Scope (the owner, 2026-11-12: "keep it to the main campus for now")

Flags and Scott Johnston / Hazelwood Hall in the west to Genesee Street and the main gate in the east. Out for now:
the athletics west of Spanjer Street, everything south of Exeter Street, the blocks north of St. John's Road.

them to the campus are the map's 31 paths; none is deleted or re-routed outside the scope, and where a hall moves
onto the grid (up to 4 m) the walk that reaches its door has its last one or two points moved to the new door, so
every walk still meets up. Checked at each step: the entity ids in those areas before and after (a diff of the
file), and a walk-through from each outside spawn to the court.

## 3. The plan

### 3.1 The approach, and why

Build the halls again from tiles and set pieces, on the grid, and keep the campus where it is.

- Windows with glass, facades with battlements, room ceilings, stair tiles, doors and room dressing all belong to
  tiles. The block halls can have none of them. `docs/SET_PIECES_PLAN.md` ("St. John's (part 2)") already
  recommends pieces on the grid for this map, and the carriageway is there.
- The grid fits: cell i covers 8i - 4 to 8i + 4 m; levels are 4 m; the halls' storeys are 4 to 4.2 m and their
  widths 16 to 20 m.
- A dorm floor then is: a row of **dorm bays** (set piece, 1 x 2 cells: 8 m of corridor with two rooms each side),
  a **stair bay** at each end and the **office bay** in the middle. A school floor is the same with classroom bays.

### 3.4 Keeping play safe

- **Spawns, flags, hills, pickups, vehicles** keep their ids and their places wherever the ground under them does
  not change. Those inside a rebuilt hall are moved by hand to the same spot of the new floor in the same step
  (listed in 1.5). The validator (`pnpm validate-map`) and MapMaker's issues list must be clean after every step.
- **Routes between the bases.** Today's three: Exeter Street, the court through the bridge's arch, and Church
  Street / Guard Path under the skybridge. All three stay open at ground level; the bridge's arch keeps its 5 m.
  Each hall keeps a door on each long side so it can still be crossed (today the open walls do that job; once they
  are closed the doors must).
- **Bots.** Every new bay gets a path test in all four turns (as `packages/shared/test/kit.test.ts` and
  `roomOptions.test.ts` do). After each step: `pnpm nav-audit` on the map; the reach probe used on 2 Nov (bots
  ordered to each floor arrive); and three seeded 24-bot TDM runs compared with the step before for bot-seconds
  indoors and kills. Room doorways are 2 m and corridors 2.4 m for the bots' 0.8 m clearance.
- **Interior depth without a maze.** About 35 dorm bays would be 140 rooms. Half are `_closed` bays (two shut doors
  a bay), so a floor has rooms to duck into without every one being a hiding place.
- **Performance** (HANDOFF, "St. John's performance": the owner's recording was 25 fps on High with 104 in a
  Roulette match; characters were the cost, then 11.5 ms scene, 6.7 ms shadows, about 1,540 draw calls). Before
  step 1 and after every step, the same measurement: `tools/perf/perf96.mjs` on st_johns (Medium, 48 bots: draws,
  shadow casters, clump), load time, and collision boxes (3,915 today, the most of any map). Budgets: tiles 1,500
  (a full rebuild is about 450), props 1,500 (733 today; the dorm rooms add about 600), prop models 80, lights 256
  (73 today; one a room would add 140, so rooms share a corridor light and get a fixture with no light), active
  pieces 300 (doors are still props, so none are added). Tiles merge into a few meshes a level and the flicker
  clipping drops hidden faces, so walls should cost less than today's blocks; props and glass panes are what to
  watch. If a step costs more than 10 % in draws or casters, it does not land until it is brought back.
- **Reduce flashing:** stained glass, the rose window and lamp globes are steady; nothing new blinks.
- **The owner asked for a new recording** after the last performance work; the first one after step 1 lands is the
  baseline for this rework on real hardware.

## 5. What I could not determine

- **Floor levels and counts** are measured; **wall openness** is sampled against wall lines I fitted by eye
  (within a metre). The stair count (21 runs, 271 blocks) is from a pattern search and was checked against the
  pictures for the halls; a stair of another shape would have been missed (I saw none).
- **Building names** are not in the file: no entity has a name. They are from the map's description and
  `docs/references/ST_JOHNS.md`, matched by position.
- **Whether a tile hall costs fewer draw calls than the block hall it replaces** is expected from how levels are
  merged, not measured: step 2 measures it.
- **Glass in an arched opening:** I read that glass panes are boxes; whether a stepped arch can be glazed without
  code I did not test.

  in detail.
- No picture from inside a hall. The 3D pictures are from outside and above; MapMaker's plan view gives floors
