1. **The DeKoven–Welles corner as it really is** (photos 1 and 2 are that corner from two sides): rebuild our corner
   to match, including what stands outside it.
2. **The clock tower gets a way into the mess hall** as well (today it has none).
3. **One external door is in the wrong place** (the owner: "i was wrong about where one of the external doors go"):
   this corrects the 2026-11-20 instruction that put the double-arched main entrance on the right-hand wall of the
   inside corner. Which door moves, and where to, is to be read from the photos; **ask the owner if the photos do
   not settle it**, do not guess.
4. **Outside omissions** ("glaring external ommisions"): compare each photo with our map from the same spot and list
   what we lack (turrets, the one-storey porches, buttresses, round windows, the cairn, the lamp pillar, the notice
   kiosk, paths), then add what MapMaker's pieces can make.
5. **DeKoven's 2nd floor hall continues round the corner to join the other dorm hall.** Remove the two dorm rooms
   that block the way to make the passage.
6. **The main entrance in photo 5 gets two stairs up to the 2nd floor, one each side, mirrored** (ours has one).

## The photos, described

**Photo 1 (the corner, from the court side).** Two three-storey wings of rough squared limestone meeting at an
inside corner, with a crenellated parapet stepping up over the corner bay. Every window has a red brick surround
with brick quoins; ground-floor windows are wider, with many small panes; the corner bay's second-floor windows
have shallow arched heads. At the foot of the inside corner, in the left wing's wall close to the corner, a small
single door in an arched opening. On the right wing, a projecting stone entrance porch a storey and a half high:
a wide pointed arch with dark double doors and lanterns inside, stepped buttresses either side of it, a sloped
stone coping, two blank stone shields beside the arch. A tall conifer stands in the court in front; low shrubs
along the walls; a narrow path runs to the small corner door.

**Photo 2 (a keep-like end block, from outside).** A square three-storey block with a round crenellated turret at
each front corner rising a storey above the parapet, narrow slit windows in the turrets, paired and single windows
with red brick surrounds on each floor. Against its front, a one-storey projecting entrance bay with its own
crenellated top and a pointed-arch door; a sign bracket beside the door. In front: a small timber notice kiosk with
a shingled roof, a bin, a concrete walk curving past. In the foreground, a low wall and pier of rounded fieldstones
of mixed colours, the pier carrying a bronze plaque and a globe lamp in an iron cage.

**Photo 3 (the outside corner with the cairn, looking along one face).** In the middle of an asphalt drive, on a
kerbed planting island with flowers: a tall tapering monument of rounded fieldstones, pointed-arch shaped, with a
pointed opening near the top holding a flame-shaped lamp, and a plaque lower down. Behind it the building's outside
corner: an octagonal or round crenellated turret a storey above the parapet, with slit windows; the wall to its
right has a round window with a quatrefoil in a brick ring high up, ordinary windows below, a hall name sign, and a
small one-storey porch with a red brick door surround; short bollards with chain along the planting bed.

**Photo 4 (the same outside corner from the other side).** A long front, two storeys over a raised ground floor,
crenellated all along. From left: a main pointed-arch double door with steps; then a run of bays divided by stepped
buttresses, each with a tall arched upper window and a smaller window below, and one round window; then, at the
corner, a square tower a storey taller than the front with slit windows; a one-storey crenellated side porch with
a red brick arched door stands against the tower, next to the fieldstone cairn on its kerbed island with flowers.
A walk leads from the drive to the main door; a small stone marker stands on the lawn.

**Photo 5 (a main entrance, from the court).** On the left, a three-storey dorm wing with red brick window
surrounds. In the middle, standing forward of the wings, a gate tower with two crenellated turrets a storey above
the roof. At its foot, a pointed-arch door at ground level; above the door, at second-floor height, a stone
landing with a crenellated parapet, reached from outside. The owner: this entrance has **two stairs up to that
second-floor landing, one on each side, mirrored**. A herringbone brick path with lamp posts leads to it; a second
pointed-arch entrance shows further right in the next wing.
