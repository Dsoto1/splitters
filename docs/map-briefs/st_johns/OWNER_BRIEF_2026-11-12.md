# St. John's: the owner's brief for a rework (2026-11-12)

The owner knows the real campus. This is what they said and what their ten reference photos show. Nothing is built
from it yet; more references are coming ("I'll add reference more after this"). The photos are not in the repository
(they are the school's own pictures, not ours to ship or commit): ask the owner for them, or look in the session
scratchpad `stjohns-refs/` while that session lives. Use them as reference only: nothing from them goes into a texture.

## What the owner said

- "St. John's map seems to use old custom stairs." (Replace them with the current stair pieces.)
- "There are many buildings with wide open walls. The windows could definitely be improved."
- The barracks / dorms: "each floor would have a long hallway down the middle, with rooms on either side (rooms had a
  desk and shelves for 2 cadets, and a bunk bed. Each room had a window, a sink, and 2 small/slim closets to left and
  right behind when walking in the room. Each room has a big thick wooden door with cast iron hardware."
- "The main entry way to campus is not a skywalk, that is seen elsewhere in real campus."
- The school building: "multi level building with hall down middle and classrooms on either side."
- References also given for the admin building's inside, the mess hall, and "some of the grounds in the center
  between library and chapel".

## What the photos show (ten, described so the build does not depend on the files)

1. **Dorm room, two views.** A small room: a sink in a wood vanity just inside, a wood desk with a hutch and a
   chair, a tall single sash window with a deep sill, a wood bunk bed (dark bedding) against the side wall, speckled
   grey floor, plain painted walls.
2. **Gym, entrance end.** A brick end wall about one storey high under a barrel-vault roof on steel trusses, a
   double-door opening in its middle leading to a lit corridor, a red banner over the door, sports banners along the
   wall top, a balcony rail above the brick, a maple court floor.
3. **Gym, the court.** A full basketball court under the same long arched truss roof, rows of hanging lights, glass
   block windows down both long walls, a flag on the far wall, bleacher-height brick side walls.
4. **The chapel from the grounds.** A long grey limestone building with a row of tall arched windows between
   buttresses, a crenellated parapet, low shrubs along its foot, a lawn with mature trees in front, a dark stone
   class marker in the grass.
5. **A stone building's side path.** Rough coursed limestone walls, arched and square windows with stone surrounds,
   a concrete path curving along the wall, shrubs and evergreens.
6. **A classroom in use.** Pennants round the top of the walls, a chalkboard and a screen on the front wall, loose
   desks and tables, wood trim.
7. and 8. **Classrooms, empty (the same pair twice).** A bright room, rows of single student desks with blue chairs,
   fluorescent strip lights, a wall of large sash windows on one side, a teacher's desk and screen at the front, a
   polished tan floor.
9. **A stone monument on the grounds.** A tall fieldstone cairn with an arched niche near its top, on a stone base in
   a mulched island where paths meet; a square crenellated limestone tower with a round window behind it.
10. **The admin building inside.** Dark wood wainscot and door frames, a heavy panelled wood door with a diamond
    pattern, a half-glazed office door, framed pictures, a wood plank ceiling, an office seen through interior windows.

## What it asks of MapMaker (pieces first: no one-off map code)

- A dorm room as a piece: door, sink, desk with shelves, bunk, window, two slim closets either side of the door; and
  a dorm floor laid out as a hall down the middle with rooms both sides. The same hall-and-rooms plan with a classroom
  piece for the school building, on several floors.
- A thick wooden door with iron hardware; better windows (tall sash; arched with stone surrounds for the chapel and
  the stone halls); walls closed where buildings stand open today.
- Current stairs in place of the old custom ones.
- The main entrance without a skywalk (find where the real skywalk is and whether the map has it there).
- The gym as a barrel-vaulted hall with a brick entrance wall; the grounds between library and chapel as lawn, trees,
  paths and the stone monument.

## Before building

Wait for the owner's further references. Then: an inventory of St. John's today (which buildings stand open, where
the old stairs are, what the entrance is), a list of the pieces to add to MapMaker, and the owner's go on the plan.
St. John's is the largest map (about 3,400 entities) and has had performance work (HANDOFF, "St. John's
performance"): measure before and after.
