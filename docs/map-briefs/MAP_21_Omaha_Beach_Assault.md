# MAP 21 — OMAHA-INSPIRED BEACH ASSAULT

**Status:** Proposed visual concept; not approved geometry. **Approved 2026-11-07 as Iron Coast: `MAP_21_Iron_Coast.md`** (the layout on the reference image, and the Breakthrough mode).
**Map scale:** Massive battlefield. **Target combatants:** 64–96.
**Recommended modes:** Asymmetric objective assault (custom rules may be needed), TDM, Co-op.

## Environment and setting
A wartime fortified beach with steep coastal cliffs. The setting must read through its buildings, landforms,
materials and believable site organization, not through decorative labels.

## Authoritative layout identity
- **Topology:** a strong linear progression from shoreline to inland.
- **Terrain and elevation:** an open beach low, bluffs high, and a bunker network on the crest.
- **Required landmarks:** landing sector, sea wall, cliff ascent trenches, gun emplacements, inland village, command
  bunker.
- **Traversable routes:** several routes from beach to cliff top: a ravine, a zigzag trench, a bunker breach, cliff
  stairs.
- **Combat-space variety:** an exposed beach at the start, balanced by smoke and cover; close-quarters bunker
  clearing; mid-range fighting inland.
- **Movement opportunities:** foot assault, cover-based progression, and optional transport depending on what can be
  implemented. Use these only where the geography and Trigger's supported mechanics justify them.
- **Signature identity:** an asymmetric attacker push through successive defensive lines.

## Explicit anti-repetition constraints
- Do not mirror bases.
- No circular arena.
- No decorative bridge network.
- Do not reuse the overhead silhouette, mirrored placement, water distribution or radial bridge network of Sunken
  Kingdom or The Spillway.
- No filler architecture.

## Gameplay plausibility / developer review
- Give several viable ascents and fair attacker staging; never force a single lethal choke.
- Routes must be physically connected and sized for the intended player count.
- Spawns, weapon pickups, objectives and exact vehicle placements are **not yet specified**. Do not invent them or
  depict their markers.
