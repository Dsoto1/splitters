# MAP 03 — FROSTBITE STATION

**Status:** Proposed visual concept; not approved geometry.
**Map scale:** Standard arena. **Target combatants:** 8–16. **Recommended modes:** DM, TDM, Elimination.

## Environment and setting
An arctic weather and research station. The setting must be readable through its buildings, landforms, materials
and believable site organization, not through decorative labels.

## Authoritative layout identity
- **Topology:** a compact offset courtyard cluster enclosed by snowbanks.
- **Terrain and elevation:** wind-scoured low rises, buried service trenches, a two-storey lab wing.
- **Required landmarks:** main lab, communications annex, snow vehicle garage, fuel tanks, radar shelter, ice cave.
- **Traversable routes:** interconnected indoor rooms with three outdoor flanks; one tunnel under the central yard.
- **Combat-space variety:** short fights inside the labs, mid range across the yard, a brief long sightline from the
  radar ridge.
- **Movement opportunities:** doors, ladders and snow ramps; no jump pads required. Use these only where the
  geography and Trigger's supported mechanics justify them.
- **Signature identity:** a claustrophobic alternation of interior and outdoor fighting in deep snow.

## Explicit anti-repetition constraints
- No river.
- No suspension bridges.
- No symmetrical split-base pattern.
- No isolated pods.
- Do not reuse the overhead silhouette, mirrored placement, water distribution or radial bridge network of Sunken
  Kingdom or The Spillway.
- No filler architecture.

## Gameplay plausibility / developer review
- Most structures share walls or protected doorways.
- Make outdoor crossings short and rich in cover.
- Routes must be physically connected and sized for the intended player count.
- Spawns, weapon pickups, objectives and exact vehicle placements are **not yet specified**; do not invent them or
  draw their markers.
