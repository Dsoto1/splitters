## What stands (the rebuild)
- **Ground**: a 320 × 320 m terrain sculpted as a gorge. The dam crosses its narrowest point; the lake lies behind it,
  the river runs south from its foot. Levels: the river's floor (0-4 m), the toe terrace under the dam (4 m), the
  west bench (8 m), the south-east bench (4 m), the east bench (20 m), both abutments and the crest (28 m).
- **Dam**: three concrete blocks, 168 × 16 m, the crest at 28 m; three intake towers behind it; the lake at 25 m.
- **Ways up**: two concrete stair towers against the dam's face (x −40 and 40), a door from the toe terrace, six
  flights, a deck and a slab onto the crest; an open hillside stair of four flights from the terrace to the east bench;
  the abutments are walked onto from the crest; the west bench is walked onto from the terrace.
- **Buildings** (set pieces): the powerhouse (Power Core Hall) on the toe terrace between the towers; a loading dock
  on the west bench; a bunker on the east bench.
- **Vegetation**: Temperate woods painted with the environment brush on the valley's grass (about 2,240 plants);
  the river's floor, the yards and the spawns kept clear.

## Spawns
- Embers (24): the west bench, x −124..−96 × z 4 and 10 (16), x −124..−112 × z −28 and −33 (8); facing east.
- Tide (24): the south-east bench, x 84..116 × z −80, −86, −92; facing north.

## Objectives
- CTF: Embers' flag (−104, 16) on the west bench; Tide's (104, −98) on the south-east bench.
- King of the Hill: the crest's middle (0, 28, 62); the toe terrace (−22, 30); the river ford (0, −40).

## Pickups
Small health at both stair towers' feet and on both river banks; large health on the east bench; the sniper rifle and
the rocket launcher on the crest (west and east); large armour on the west abutment, small armour by the powerhouse;
a shotgun and a rifle on the toe terrace; ammunition down the river.

## Left for later

the spillway chute; a second way to the east bench; 16 more spawns for 32 v 32.
