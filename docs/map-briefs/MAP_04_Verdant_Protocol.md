# MAP 04 — VERDANT PROTOCOL

**Status:** Proposed visual concept; not approved geometry.
**Map scale:** Large arena. **Target combatants:** 16–32. **Recommended modes:** TDM, CTF, KOTH, Co-op.

## Environment and setting
An overgrown jungle research and security compound. The setting must be readable through its buildings, landforms,
materials and believable site organization, not through decorative labels.

## Authoritative layout identity
- **Topology:** a loose, irregular campus threaded through dense vegetation.
- **Terrain and elevation:** rolling red-earth slopes, shallow dry gullies, low bunker entrances.
- **Required landmarks:** command building, greenhouse lab, crashed supply craft, old watchpost, underground
  quarantine bunker.
- **Traversable routes:** twisting footpaths, broken perimeter roads, foliage shortcuts, a subterranean connector.
- **Combat-space variety:** short sightlines in the jungle, medium ones in the courtyards, one controllable overlook.
- **Movement opportunities:** natural slopes, vines or ladders where supported, short bunker stairways. Use these
  only where the geography and Trigger's supported mechanics justify them.
- **Signature identity:** dense concealment and ambush routes; no central megastructure.

## Explicit anti-repetition constraints
- No large river.
- No bridge network.
- No perfect symmetry.
- No mandatory vehicles.
- Do not reuse the overhead silhouette, mirrored placement, water distribution or radial bridge network of Sunken
  Kingdom or The Spillway.
- No filler architecture.

## Gameplay plausibility / developer review
- Keep playable lanes clear amid the vegetation; foliage must not hide lethal collision surprises.
- Routes must be physically connected and sized for the intended player count.
- Spawns, weapon pickups, objectives and exact vehicle placements are **not yet specified**; do not invent them or
  draw their markers.
