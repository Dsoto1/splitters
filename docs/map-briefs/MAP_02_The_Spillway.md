# MAP 02 — THE SPILLWAY

**Status:** Proposed visual concept; not approved geometry.
**Map scale:** Battlefield. **Target combatants:** 32–64. **Recommended modes:** TDM, CTF, KOTH.

## Environment and setting
A hydroelectric dam carved into a mountain gorge. The setting must be readable through its buildings, landforms,
materials and believable site organization, not through decorative labels.

## Authoritative layout identity
- **Topology:** a long horizontal industrial spine with unequal service wings.
- **Terrain and elevation:** the dam crest high, the powerhouse at mid level, the downstream rocky basin low.
- **Required landmarks:** dam crest, generator hall, switchyard, intake towers, west maintenance yard, east service
  tunnel.
- **Traversable routes:** parallel dry service roads and interior turbine halls, plus a small number of deliberate
  cross-canyon connections.
- **Combat-space variety:** long range on the dam crest, close range in the turbine rooms, medium range on the
  maintenance terraces.
- **Movement opportunities:** ladders, service ramps and industrial staircases. Vehicle roads only where they are
  wide enough, and kept limited. Use these only where the geography and Trigger's supported mechanics justify them.
- **Signature identity:** an industrial elevation puzzle centred on a real dam, not islands of platforms.

## Explicit anti-repetition constraints
- Do not reproduce Sunken Kingdom's canals.
- No ornamental waterfalls throughout.
- Avoid a symmetric spiderweb of central platforms.
- Do not reuse the overhead silhouette, mirrored placement, water distribution or radial bridge network of Sunken
  Kingdom or The Spillway.
- No filler architecture.

## Gameplay plausibility / developer review
- Make the powerhouse one connected building with interior access, not a freestanding bridge island.
- Routes must be physically connected and sized for the intended player count.
- Spawns, weapon pickups, objectives and exact vehicle placements are **not yet specified**; do not invent them or
  draw their markers.
