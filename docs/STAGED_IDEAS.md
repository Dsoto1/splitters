# Staged ideas (owner backlog)

Ideas the owner has asked to hold until they say go. Each entry records what was
asked, how we read it, and open questions. Nothing here is scheduled until the
owner releases it.

## 1. Dustwater, expanded (Wild West)

**Asked (2026-10-27):** a more expansive map with more structures: mines, a
larger town with more depth. The train should come out of a tunnel beyond the
map's edge, run through the map, and vanish into another tunnel. On the other
side of the map, a second track carries a train the opposite way, tunnel to
tunnel.

**How we read it:**
- **Bigger footprint:** a deeper town with a main street plus back streets and
  alleys. Add more buildings, such as a hotel, church, bank, jail, livery,
  smithy, assay office, saloon and boarding houses, using the Low Poly Wild
  West kit (`ww_*`) already in the game. Also add ridges and a creek bed for
  long sightlines and cover.
- **Mines:** a mine complex with several entrances, walkable tunnels,
  a headframe, ore carts on rails, tailings piles and a stamp mill. At least
  one tunnel links two areas of the map as a flank route.
- **Two railroads,** one on each side of the map:
  - Each track runs from a tunnel portal at the boundary, through the map, into
    a tunnel portal on the far side.
  - Track A runs west to east; track B runs east to west.
  - Trains appear out of the tunnel, cross the map and disappear into the far
    tunnel, then repeat on a timer.
  - Tunnel mouths are outside the playable area, behind the boundary.
- **Train as a hazard and as cover:**
  - It is a solid moving obstacle that blocks lines of sight while it passes.
  - Being hit by it kills, as today's train does.
  - Optional: a warning whistle and crossing signals before it arrives.
- **Keep:** the station, water tower, the existing train and mine cart
  behaviour, and the current game modes. Re-check spawns, pickups and the
  48v48 budget (perf96 `PERF_MAX_DRAWS`).

**Open questions for the owner:**
1. **Train type:** passenger, freight, or one of each?
2. **Riding:** should players be able to ride or board the trains, or are they
   hazard and cover only?
3. **Size:** map size target, for example roughly 2× the current Dustwater,
   built for up to 48v48?
4. **Name:** keep the name Dustwater, or make it a new map and keep the
   current one?

**Status:** staged; waiting for the owner's go.
