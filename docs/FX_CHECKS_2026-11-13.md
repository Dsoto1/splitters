# Effects checks, 2026-11-13

Findings only: no effect's look was changed. Helper session of lane A, branch `wip/fx-checks`, from main 58edb4f1.
The checks cover this week's Binbun FX: loot glows (`game/lootGlow.ts`), portal and jump pad effects (`game/fixtureFx.ts`,
`game/scenarioVisuals.ts`), status auras (`game/statusFx.ts`), and the tunnel kit's lights.
Pictures were taken with SwiftShader (software rendering, no GPU), so they show placement and contrast but not real frame rates.

The owner's choices are in **Proposals** at the end.

## Summary

1. **Pantry Raid's mouse holes don't work as portals.** The 0.9 m skirting board runs across both. Nobody gets through
   by walking. Only Small characters get through by jumping. The navmesh routes bots through them, and a bot would
   get stuck at the board. The new swirl fits its frame, but it shows as pale grey-beige smoke, not a dark hole.
2. **Loot rings on bright floors are faint everywhere.** WCAG contrast is about 1.06 to 1.18 on average with Reduce
   flashing on (the default) and 1.1 to 1.45 with it off, on sand, snow, white tile and pale planks. The ammo and
   armor rings are the faintest. The flicker scene only shows glows on dark grass, where they read well.
3. **No budget is broken, and the new effects cost about nothing in draws or shadows.** `perf:budget` passes both
   builds, but it never has one of these effects in view (lasting 0). Low turns prop flames off but not loot glows
   (`decor`). On Low, cycling flame traps out of sight starve the pads and the portal of their four places, so the
   lift fades in and out.
4. **Reduce flashing passes:** 0 transitions for loot, fixtures and status with the setting on, and at most 2 a
   second in a 10° field with it off.

## 1. Pantry Raid's mouse-hole portals

The map has two portals, `mousehole_w` (0.15, 0, 28), facing +x, and `mousehole_s` (70, 0, 0.15), facing +z. Both are
2.6 x 2.4 m, colour `#3a2a1a` (dark brown). Each sits inside a painted frame made of map blocks (`block_344..346` and
`block_347..349`, 1.2 m deep, opening 2.8 x 2.6 m) and behind the room's 0.9 m skirting board (`block_015` west,
`block_014` south). The skirting board runs straight across both holes. This was first noted on 2026-11-01 (HANDOFF,
"Grip pass 2") and was never fixed.

### Do they work? (simulation, `Match` on `maps/pantry_raid.json`, scratch test, not committed)

Test: a player walks straight at each hole for 3 s. A second run does the same while jumping every 20 ticks.

| Body | Walk in | Walk + jump | Closest the body got (m from the wall) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Small (Sprout, 0.78) | no | **yes** (both holes) | 0.73 walking, 0.61 jumping |
| Standard (Ranger, 1.0) | no | no | 0.82 walking, 0.71 jumping (stopped at 0.8 m up) |
| Large (Knight, 1.18) | no | no | 0.89 walking, 0.88 jumping |

- A portal catches a body within its radius plus 0.14 m of its surface (`PORTAL_REACH`). The skirting board holds
  every body at least 0.4 m plus its radius from the wall. **Nobody can walk in.**
- Only a Small body that jumps onto the skirting board gets caught. Standard and Large bodies standing on the skirting
  board hit the painted frame's lintel (2.6 m up) with their heads.
- **Bots:** the navmesh does get a link each way (`portalLinks`: start (1.05, 0, 28) and (70, 0, 1.05)), so the
  planner routes through the holes. A bot following that link walks into the skirting board. Two 180 s matches with 12
  bots (seeds 1 and 2) gave 0 trips through a hole, and no bot came within 1.5 m of a hole's mouth. Bots don't seem to
  choose this route in practice, but a bot that does would get stuck.

### Do they read and fit? (pictures)

Offline Pantry Raid, medium graphics, 640 x 360 pictures, standing 6 m, 2 m, and at an angle from the west hole and 6 m
from the south hole, with Reduce flashing on and off. A last picture was taken with the pack swirl held back, so the
painted swirl shows.

- **Colour: it doesn't read as a mouse hole.** The pair's colour `#3a2a1a` goes through the pack's two-colour mix and
  comes out as a **pale, murky grey-beige smoke**, with darker brown in the middle and a cream rim. Up close (2 m) it
  looks like a dirty cloud or a smudged window, not a dark hole you can go through. The old painted swirl was a dark
  spiral on grey, and read more like a hole. With Reduce flashing on the cloud holds still and is a little dimmer; it
  reads the same.
- **Fit: the swirl fills the game's own stone frame (2.6 x 2.4), which sits inside the map's painted wooden frame
  (opening 2.8 x 2.6).** You see a double frame: a thin pale-tan stone strip (about 0.1 m at the sides and 0.2 m under
  the lintel) inside the brown wooden frame. The swirl doesn't overrun its frame anywhere.
- **The skirting board cuts across the bottom 0.9 m of both holes.** In every picture the white board covers the
  bottom third of the swirl, so the hole reads as a window above a ledge. This matches the simulation above.
- The portal's swirl gets its place: 1 to 6 lasting effects were running in these views; no budget pressure here.

So: the portals fit their frames, but they don't read as mouse holes, and only Small characters (by jumping) can use
them. Bots plan through them, but they couldn't get through if they tried.


## 2. Loot rings on bright floors

### Method

Offline match, medium graphics, 640 x 360. For each pickup:

1. Stand 3.5 m off and look at the base of the pickup.
2. Hide the pickup model (it spins and bobs).
3. Wait until the glow is full (0.8 with Reduce flashing off, 0.5 with it on).
4. Take a picture of a box round the base, projected from a 1.1 m circle.
5. Hold back the loot glows and take the same picture again.

"Ring pixels" are the pixels the glow makes at least 2% brighter. The ratios are WCAG luminance contrast,
(L_glow + 0.05) / (L_floor + 0.05), taken on those pixels:

- **mean**: the whole ring;
- **p90**: its brightest tenth;
- **floor L**: the floor's mean relative luminance in the box, without the glow.

For scale: WCAG 1.4.11 asks 3:1 for a UI part that matters, and 1.5:1 is roughly where a shape stops being easy to
see at a glance.

Reduce flashing is **on by default**, so the "calm" columns are what most players see.

| Map, floor | Pickup | Floor L | Ring px | calm mean / p90 | full mean / p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dustwater, pale boardwalk | armor 191 | 0.12 | 1,700–2,500 | 1.13 / 1.29 | 1.42 / 2.05 |
| Dustwater, boardwalk | armor 192 | 0.09 | 1,700 | 1.23 / 1.51 | 1.25 / 1.54 |
| Dustwater, sunlit pale planks | ammo 193 | 0.22 | 1,250–1,400 | **1.11 / 1.26** | 1.17 / 1.43 |
| Dustwater, planks | ammo 194 | 0.14 | 1,650–1,900 | **1.06 / 1.11** | 1.08 / 1.16 |
| Dustwater, sand | ammo `ccgzk7ihbe` | 0.13 | 1,350–1,500 | 1.12 / 1.30 | 1.18 / 1.47 |
| Dustwater, boardwalk | health 188 | 0.11 | 1,500–1,700 | 1.26 / 1.60 | 1.44 / 1.98 |
| Mercy Ward, white tiles | ammo 290 | **0.52** | 1,150–1,250 | **1.14 / 1.33** | 1.17 / 1.46 |
| Mercy Ward, grey floor | ammo 288 | 0.13 | 1,350–1,400 | 1.17 / 1.41 | 1.38 / 1.85 |
| Mercy Ward, grey floor | ammo 289 | 0.12 | 1,100–1,250 | 1.18 / 1.46 | 1.20 / 1.51 |
| Mercy Ward, grey floor | armor 285 | 0.23 | 1,600–1,700 | 1.15 / 1.32 | 1.22 / 1.49 |

Two Mercy Ward armor pickups (283, 284) were left out: a wall stood between the camera and the pickup.
On Whiteout Station, the pictures of one armor and one health pickup were mostly blocked and were left out (fewer than
520 ring pixels). Frost Basin timed out loading after 20 min (SwiftShader, shared machine), so Whiteout Station stood
in for snow.

**What the pictures show:**
- On snow, with Reduce flashing off, the health ring reads as a pale white-green oval. With it on, the armor ring on
  the icy blue-grey floor almost can't be seen: its blue blends with the ice.
- On pale planks and white tiles the ammo ring is a faint, slightly lighter oval. You see it only once you know where
  to look. It has no edge you can make out at 3.5 m.
- The health ring's green shows as a soft green smudge at its far side. The armor ring's blue reads best on mid-grey,
  and barely at all on pale sand.
- No ring reaches 1.5:1 on average anywhere, even on mid-tone floors. Their brightest tenth reaches about 2:1 at best,
  with Reduce flashing off.
- **The ammo ring on bright floors (L 0.2 to 0.5) is the faintest: 1.06 to 1.14 with Reduce flashing on**, which is
  the default setting.
- **Reduce flashing (on by default) takes away a third to half of what contrast there is.** Its level is 0.5 against
  0.8, for example 1.42 down to 1.13 on armor 191. So the faint case is the usual case.
- The glow adds light, so on a bright floor it can only add a little: white plus a pale glow stays near white.
  Lifting the level on its own won't fix the ammo ring there. It needs a darker or more saturated edge.

## 3. Performance, Medium and Low, with and without the new effects

"Without" is a scratch build of this commit with one extra line in `LastingFx.update`: lasting effects whose key is
`loot.*`, `portal`, `pad` or `status.*` are dropped, so their plain looks stand in. The build is in this session's
scratchpad and was never committed. All runs used SwiftShader on a shared 4-core machine; the load average was 7 to 9
from other sessions. Frame times are only good for comparison within this table, and even then roughly.

### `pnpm perf:budget` (Frost Basin, 96 players, medium and low, 4 s a scenario)

| Build | Graphics | Spread draws / casters | Clump draws / casters | Lasting effects drawn | Slowest effect start |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| with | medium | 409 / 58 | 293 / 25 | 0 | 0.5 ms |
| without | medium | 249 / 52 | 144 / 23 | 0 | 0.7 ms |
| with | low | 236 / 0 | 226 / 0 | 0 | 0.6 ms |
| without | low | 213 / 0 | 193 / 0 | 0 | 0.9 ms |

- **No budget is broken, in either build:** the draw budget is 900 (worst 293), shadow casters 600 (worst 25), and
  an effect start 40 ms (worst 0.9 ms). No effect is in the shadow pass (`effectCasters` 0 everywhere).
- Starting each new effect for the first time costs 0 to 0.1 ms (`loot.*`, `portal`, `pad`, `status.*`): the
  shaders are built while the match loads.
- **The run doesn't measure the new effects.** `lasting` was 0 in every scenario: Frost Basin has no portals, and
  neither scenario's camera had a pad or a pickup within range. The machine also drew only 1 to 3 frames per sample
  window (0.1 to 0.5 fps). So the draw differences between the builds (409 against 249) come from where the bots
  happened to be, not from the effects. As a gate, `perf:budget` can't see these effects at all.

### Targeted views on Pantry Raid (scratch driver, 3 bots, Reduce flashing on, 8 s a view after 6 s to settle)

The views:

- **portal+loot**: (12, 0, 26), looking at the west mouse hole, with pickups in range.
- **pads+loot**: (79, 0, 33), looking at the two counter pads, which sit behind the toaster.
- **status**: three bots 3.5 m ahead, burning, poisoned and slowed.

| portal+loot | medium | 125 / 123 | 40 / 45 | 5.5 / 0.3 | 459 / 492 |
| pads+loot | medium | 104 / 102 | 9 / 9 | 3.9 / 1.7 | 381 / 426 |
| status | medium | 168 / 182 | 12 / 12 | 3.3 / 1.3 | 498 / 641 |

- **Cost: within noise.** With 4 to 6 of the new effects drawn, draw calls change by -14 to +3 against the other build,

  them casts a shadow. Frame time in SwiftShader on a loaded machine swings by up to 2x between runs of the same
  build, so these runs show no measurable frame-time cost. That still needs a real-GPU run.
- No new effect costs a draw-call or shadow budget anything worth counting.

### What Low turns off, and what it doesn't

| Effect | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lasting places in all (`QUALITY.loops`) | 4 | 10 | 16 |
| Prop flames (`decor`) | **off** (`PropFlames` enabled = quality ≠ low) | on | on |
| Loot glows (`decor`) | **on**, at most 2 (half of 4) | at most 5 | at most 8 |
| Portal swirl, pad lift (`fixture`) | on, after effects of the moment | on | on |
| Status auras (tier 0) | on, at most 3 characters | 3 | 3 |
| Reduce flashing calming | same on every quality | | |

- **The loot glows are `decor`, like prop flames, but Low doesn't turn them off.** The `content/vfx.json` comment says
  "`decor` ones … aren't drawn on low graphics", but that's only true of prop flames (`propFlames.ts`); `lootGlow`
  has no quality check. Either the comment or the code is wrong. The HANDOFF note (2 / 5 / 8 by quality) suggests
  keeping glows on Low was intended.
- **On Low, Pantry Raid's oven burners starve the fixtures.** This came up in the pads+loot view. The four burner jets
  (`fire.trap`, tier 0, on 2 s / off 3 s) were inside the closed oven, 22 to 30 m away and out of sight. They still
  claim lasting places, because `LastingFx` sorts by distance only and has no visibility check. A stopped jet keeps
  counting against `loops` while it finishes (0.2 to 0.6 s). So with 4 places in all, `room = loopRoom + live.size`
  is often 1 or 0, and the pads and portal get a place for a moment, lose it, then get it back.
  - Live sets sampled every 2 s on Low read `[trap]`, `[trap, trap]`, `[trap, pad_174]`, `[trap]`. On Medium the
    same view keeps both pads and the portal steady, and only the loot glows swap.
  - Seen in play: on Low the pad lift fades in and out every few seconds, and with Reduce flashing on (rise 0.3 a
    second) it never gets past a faint start. This isn't a seizure risk (slow, low contrast), but it reads as a glitch.
  - Any map with cycling flame traps or vents will do this on Low.
- Status auras on Low: 3 of the 4 places, as designed. With three status characters in view, a fire next to them
  still gets the fourth place, but portals, pads and loot glows get none. That is the intended order.

## 4. Reduce flashing: `E2E_ONLY=flashing node e2e/run.mjs`

Run on ports 4953/4954 on this commit's build: **all e2e checks passed** (exit 0). The gate allows at most 6
transitions a second over a quarter of the screen (WCAG 2.3.1).

|---|---|---|---|

| fixtures (portal pair + pad, 5 m) | 0, 0 | 0, **2** | 0.022–0.049 |
| status (burn, poison, slow; 3.5 m) | 0, 0 | 0, 0 | 0.012–0.024 |
| tunnel (day scene: out of a lit tunnel and back) | 3, **4** | 3, 4 | 0.038–0.195 |
| blast (older; for scale) | 4, **9** | 5, 8 | 0.010–0.246 |
| alarm (older; for scale) | 0, 0 | 9, 9 | 0.026–0.089 |

What the recorded frame strips (`e2e-artifacts/flashing-<moment>-{on,off}.png`) show:

- **loot:** on dark grass every ring reads plainly: the green health ovals and the gold weapon ovals, with the blue
  armor shape smaller. Calm and full look almost the same. This is the opposite of section 2. **The flicker scene
  tests the glows only on a dark floor at night, where they read best.**
- **fixtures, off:** the pad lift throws bright white-blue spikes that change from frame to frame, and the portal goes
  from a turning swirl to a flat bright cyan sheet as the pack effect takes over. That makes 2 transitions a second
  in a 10° field, inside the limit. **With the setting on**, the lift is a still, dimmer column and the swirl a still
  gate: 0.
- **status, off:** the burning bot's aura has white-hot cores and yellow licks that change every frame. They're small
  on screen, so there are 0 transitions. With the setting on, they run at half speed and look dimmer.
- **tunnel:** the brightness step out of the tunnel into daylight and back is 3 transitions a second whatever the
  setting, because it is scene light, not an effect. It's inside the limit, but it's the biggest step of any of this
  week's moments (0.279 of a quarter screen in one frame). Nothing in the tunnel lights themselves blinks.
- **Not gated:** WCAG's own area is a quarter of any 10° field. The flow reports it but doesn't fail on it. With the
  setting on, the older `blast` moment is at 9 a second there. None of this week's effects is above 2.
- **Not covered by the flow:** chill and frozen auras (checked by hand only, per HANDOFF), any effect on Low, and the
  Low churn from section 3 (a pad lift fading in and out every few seconds; slow, so no flash).

## Proposals for the owner

Pick one letter per question. (★ = recommended.)

**1. Pantry Raid's mouse holes: can't be used (skirting board)**
- a. ★ In MapMaker: split the skirting board at each hole, so it stops at the hole's frame and the portals work for
  every body size and for bots. It's a map edit only; the game's code doesn't change.
- b. Same as (a), and also lower the painted frame's lintel 0.2 m to meet the stone frame, so there's one frame and no
  pale strip.
- c. Leave them as a Small-only secret, and remove the navmesh link for portals a standard body can't reach (a code
  change in `portalLinks`).
- d. Leave as is.

**2. Pantry Raid's mouse holes: how they look**
- a. ★ A per-portal option in MapMaker, "Swirl: pack / painted", with the mouse holes set to painted (the dark
  spiral). Other portals keep the pack swirl.
- b. Keep the pack swirl and tint the mouse holes a darker, more saturated colour so the mix comes out dark brown or
  black.
- c. Leave as is.

**3. Loot rings on bright floors**
- a. ★ Give the rings a thin dark outline (a second, multiplied ring under the additive one), so they read on pale
  floors and stay the same on dark ones. Reduce flashing keeps its level, since it's not brightness that's added.
- b. Raise Reduce flashing's loot level from 0.5 to 0.65 (still eased in and held still), and the ammo ring's own
  brightness. That helps mid floors but barely changes white ones.
- c. Saturate the ammo ring (pale → a light cyan or orange) so it differs from white floors by colour.
- d. Leave as is.

**4. Loot glows on Low graphics**
- a. ★ Keep them on Low (2 at most) and correct the `content/vfx.json` comment, which says decor isn't drawn on Low.
- b. Turn them off on Low like prop flames, which frees 2 of the 4 places for pads and portals.

**5. Lasting places on Low (effects out of sight take the places)**
- a. ★ Rank lasting effects behind the camera, or hidden by a closed door or oven, after the fixtures in view (a cheap
  frustum test in `LastingFx.update`), and don't count a stopping effect against new starts of a higher tier.
- b. Raise Low's lasting places from 4 to 6.
- c. Leave as is.

**6. Checks that would have caught these**
- a. ★ Add a bright-floor moment to the flicker scene (pickups on white tiles by day) and a "rings visible" check
  (ring contrast at least 1.3 with the setting on). Add a `perf:budget` scenario with pads, a portal and pickups in
  view, so the gate sees these effects.
- b. Only the `perf:budget` scenario.
- c. Neither.
