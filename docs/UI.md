# UI and design system

The menus, HUD and MapMaker follow the UI reference board: deep navy panels,
cyan for selection, orange and team colours for emphasis, condensed uppercase
headings and controller-style hint bars. All art in the UI is our own: the
logo is CSS type, menu backdrops and portraits are rendered live from our 3D
characters, and map previews are drawn from each map's real geometry. Nothing
is generated by AI.

| Categories (MapMaker palette) | `--cat-structure` cyan, `--cat-props` orange, `--cat-nature` success green, `--cat-terrain` `--earth #c9a36b`, `--cat-gameplay` pink, `--cat-npcs` error red, `--cat-vehicles` info blue, `--cat-objects` purple, `--cat-lights` warning yellow, `--cat-paint` highlight indigo |

Type: **Chakra Petch** (display: headings, buttons, numbers) and **Barlow**
(body). Both are SIL OFL fonts bundled through `@fontsource`.

Shared components: `.panel`/`.panel-title`, `.tabs` with `.bumper` keys,
`.hintbar` with `kbd.a/.b/.x/.y` (face-button colours), `.stat-bar`, `.stepper`.

## Screens

- **Front end** (`src/frontend/frontEnd.ts`) has these screens:
  - **Home:** logo, slanted nav, player badge with portrait, featured map, news strip.
  - **Arcade:** map cards with the map's picture on top and its top-down plan beside the name (two
    separate boxes, `mapThumbs.ts`); the setup panel shows the picture and the plan side by side; mode,
    bots, skill, score and time. The featured map, the host's Change map picker and the game browser
    (listings on built-in maps) show them the same way. See CONTENT_PIPELINE.md, Official maps.
  - **Characters:** a portrait grid with Q/E filters and an info panel.
  - **Online** and **Options.**

  Your character stands on a lit plinth behind every screen (`menuScene.ts`).
- **Character Designer** (`src/designer/`): Body / Head / Outfit / Colours tabs (Q/E), R randomises, a "Start from" portrait grid.
- **HUD** (`src/game/hud.ts`):
  - top left: a rotating circular minimap (`minimap.ts`);
  - top centre: the scorebar (TDM team totals, or you vs the leader in Deathmatch);
  - top right: the kill feed;
  - bottom left: your portrait with health and armour bars;
  - bottom right: the weapon with magazine and reserve ammo;
  - centre: a target name and health bar after a hit.
- **MapMaker** (`src/editor/`), laid out after the board's build and logic views:
  - **Top bar:** map name, file actions (icons), undo/redo, the validation pill, the workspace tabs
    **Build / Entities / Logic / Environment / Test**, then Grid, Snap and Theme, bots, skill and ▶ TEST.
  - **Opening and quitting:** MapMaker always opens on a blank starter map (one room and a spawn, the
    same as **New**); nothing is restored on its own. **Open…** lists, in order, unsaved drafts, saved
    maps and the templates; **Import** loads a `.cmap.json` file. While a map has unsaved changes it is
    kept as a draft in browser storage (the five most recent maps), and Open… offers it as
    "Unsaved draft (*name*, *time*)"; switching maps with New, Open… or Import keeps the draft rather
    than asking. **Save** (Ctrl+S) stores the map under the name in the top bar and drops its draft.
    **◀ Quit** goes back to the main menu; with unsaved changes it first asks in a small dialog:
    **Save & quit** (a map that was never saved is named there first), **Quit without saving** (drops
    the draft) or **Cancel**.
  - **Palette** (320 px; 300 px at 1400 px and below; `palette.ts`, grouping in `paletteCatalog.ts`):
    - **Tabs**, one colour per category (the `--cat-*` tokens: on the tab, the group heads and a thin
      line under every card; cyan still marks the active piece). Build: **Tiles** and **Walls**
      (structure), **Props**, **Nature**, **Terrain**; Entities: **Gameplay** (spawns, game-mode
      objectives, pickups by kind), **NPCs** (squads, spawners), **Vehicles** (vehicles, turrets,
      parked vehicles, race track), **Objects** (doors, logic and triggers, lifts, platforms,
      destructibles, traps), **Lights** (lights, liquids); Environment: **Paint**, **Nature**, **Terrain**.
    - **Groups** fold open and closed (remembered per browser) and show how many pieces they hold.
      Props are sorted into themes (Industrial & Military, Home & Town, Sci-Fi, Medieval & Gothic,
      Dungeons & Caves, Wild West, Ancient & Jungle), then by their own category; only the first
      theme starts open. Trees, rocks and plants are under Nature with the scatter brush.
    - **Search** (`/` focuses it) ranks matches from every tab (`paletteSearch.ts`): each word must
      match the name, the tags (category, theme, what it is, the Style Packs that use it) or the pack
      the piece comes from, as a word start, inside a word, with one typo, or as an abbreviation of
      the name ("sbw" → Sandbag Wall); name matches rank first. Enter picks the best match, ↓ moves
      into the results, Esc clears.
    - **Favourites / Recent**: right-click a piece, press F on it or click its ★ to keep it in the
      row under the search box; Recent lists the last pieces picked (both per browser).
    - **Grid view** (the default): four pictures to a row, names in a card beside the palette on
      hover or focus; the button by the search box switches to cards with names, three to a row.
      Arrow keys move between pieces, Home/End jump within a group, Enter/Space picks.
    - **Pictures** of tiles, props and vehicles come from prebuilt atlases (`public/thumbs/`, see
      CONTENT_PIPELINE.md, Palette pictures), one image per group loaded the first time one of its
      pieces is shown (`paletteAtlas.ts`); only a piece missing from the atlas is rendered at runtime
      (`ModelThumbnails`). Icons are drawn as SVG.
    - **Style Pack** picker under the search narrows every tab, the search and the favourites to one pack.
  - **Right panel:** Map properties (the map's picture beside its plan, **Set thumbnail** / **Clear**, name,
    theme, size, players, mode, map layers, then backdrop, blood and boundary), or the inspector for the
    selection. Set thumbnail (`makerThumb.ts`) dims everything but a 16:9 frame over the view; move the
    camera as usual, then Capture (Enter) or Cancel (Esc). Editing aids (grid, ghosts, outlines, spawn
    markers) are hidden for the capture; undo works on it like any other edit. The Open dialog shows each
    draft, saved map and template with its picture and plan (`libraryThumbs.ts`).
  - **Over the view:** a tool bar (Select, Place, Erase, Paint, Walls, Terrain, Rotate, Delete; icon, name
    and key on each, the active tool lit cyan, Place green, Erase red), a one-line hint for the active tool,
    a level strip (Level N / Ground / Basement, up and down), a hover card by the pointer (name, size,
    theme, connectors) and an X/Y/Z gizmo on the selection. Select outlines its target in cyan, Erase in
    red; the level being edited has a cyan grid and the others are drawn ghosted.
  - Below 1100 px the right panel folds away and the palette narrows (three pictures to a row below
    900 px); the tabs wrap rather than scroll, and nothing in the palette scrolls sideways.
- **Options**: crosshair styles (cross, dot, circle, chevron, gap) and colours (`src/game/crosshair.ts`).
- **Weapon icons** in the kill feed and the character loadout are rendered side-on from the weapons'
  own models (`src/frontend/weaponIcons.ts`, `modelThumbs.ts`); MapMaker's palette atlases are rendered
  by the same renderer at build time.

Pickup and objective notices are boxed toasts with a coloured edge (green pickups, orange messages).

Portraits come from `PortraitRenderer` (`src/frontend/portraits.ts`): a private
scene renders each look to a 256 px texture once and caches the PNG data URL.
