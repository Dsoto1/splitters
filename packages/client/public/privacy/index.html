Privacy Policy

Trigger, a browser game by ToshiroPlay, at trigger.toshiroplay.com. Effective 27 October 2026.

This policy explains what Trigger collects and why. We keep it to the minimum the game needs. Trigger is free, plays in your browser, and never requires an account. We use no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and we never sell, rent or share your information.

Playing without signing in

Offline matches, MapMaker and the character designer run entirely in your browser. Your settings, saved setups and the maps you make are kept in your browser's own storage on your device. We do not receive them.

Signing in with Google (optional)

Signing in is only needed for features tied to an account, such as managing a server you host. When you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares the following with us through Amazon Cognito, our sign-in service:

  • your Google account identifier (a number that tells accounts apart)
  • your name
  • your email address

We ask only for the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We cannot see your password, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

What other players see

The player name you choose, your character, the games you host or join, and what happens in a match. Your email address is never shown to other players. Trigger has no chat.

Online matches run in a player's browser or on a player's computer and connect players directly (peer to peer). As with any direct connection, players in a match can see each other's IP addresses, unless the connection goes through our relay.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to sign you in, list and connect games, keep matches fair and prevent abuse. We do not use it for advertising and we do not send marketing email.

Where it is stored

Trigger's servers run on Amazon Web Services in the United States (us-east-1): Amazon Lightsail for the site and game browser, and Amazon Cognito for sign-in. Browsers use public STUN servers (for example Google's) to find a direct connection; those see your IP address. The game's fonts and files are served from our own server.

Cookies and your browser

Trigger sets no cookies of its own. If you sign in, the sign-in service at login.toshiroplay.com uses cookies needed for signing in and out, and nothing else. The game uses your browser's storage for your settings and your sign-in session; you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in: nothing about you is kept beyond the short-lived items above.
  • Sign out at any time from the main menu.
  • To see or delete your account and everything tied to it, email us from the address you sign in with (below). We will act on your request within 30 days.
  • You can remove Trigger's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

Trigger's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Trigger is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game.
