Privacy

Trigger, a browser game by ToshiroPlay (trigger.toshiroplay.com). Last updated 27 October 2026.

Trigger is free to play in your browser, and playing never needs an account. We collect as little as we can: no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or share your data.

What we collect, and why

WhatWhyHow long
Your Google account ID and name (and the email address Google shares), only if you choose Sign in To know it is you when you use features that need an account (for example, managing a server you host). Sign-in is optional. Held by the sign-in service (Amazon Cognito, run for ToshiroPlay) while you have an account. Your browser keeps the sign-in only for the open tab. We keep no other user records.
Your IP address Needed to connect you to games and to our servers, and used briefly to limit abuse (rate limits). Kept in memory only while needed (minutes). Server logs, which can include addresses, are deleted after at most 14 days.
Game settings (name, controls, graphics, maps you make) So the game remembers your choices. Stored in your own browser (local storage), not on our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them.
Game listings (game name, host name, map, player count) when you list a game you host To show your game in the game browser. Removed when you stop hosting, or about a minute after your game stops checking in.

Playing with others

Online games run in a player's browser or on a player's computer and connect players directly (peer to peer). Like any direct connection, the players in a match can see each other's IP addresses, unless the connection goes through our relay. Trigger has no chat. The names you choose are shown to the other players.

Cookies

Trigger sets no cookies of its own. If you sign in, the sign-in service (login.toshiroplay.com) uses cookies needed for signing in and out, and nothing else.

Services we use

  • Amazon Web Services (hosting, and Amazon Cognito for sign-in), and Google as the sign-in provider you choose.
  • Public STUN servers (for example Google's) that help browsers find a direct connection: they see your IP address.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in: nothing about you is stored beyond the short-lived items above.
  • Sign out at any time from the main menu.
  • Ask us to delete your account, or ask what we hold about you, using the contact below.

Children

Trigger is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.
