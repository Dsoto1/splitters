Airliner cabin kit (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, lofted rings and flat sheets) by
tools/content/prepare-cabin.mjs with tools/content/pieces.mjs, which write
the GLB files in this folder. Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same
code writes the same files.

The fuselage sections follow the cross-section of the owner's Meshy airliner
shell (prop airliner_shell), measured into fuselage.json here by
tools/content/fuselage-profile.mjs: a table of ring radii, our own
measurement, holding none of the download's geometry or texture.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props/tiletrigger-cabin.json and the provenance
ledger).

Pieces: nose and flight deck, door section, straight section, short section,
over-wing exit section, tail with rear doors; economy seats (triple, pair),
business seat, pilot seat, overhead bins (long, short), galley, lavatory,
cabin divider, boarding stairs.
