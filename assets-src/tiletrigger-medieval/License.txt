Style Pack pieces: 10 Medieval Village (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres, rings and flat sheets) by
tools/content/prepare-medieval.mjs with tools/content/pieces.mjs, which write
the GLB files in this folder. Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same
code writes the same files.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props.json and the provenance ledger).

Pieces: stone well, market awning, market pavilion, shop sign, hay cart,
blacksmith's forge, workbench, flower planter, tarpaulined crates, chimney
stack, tarp tent, wattle fence, spiked fence, church bell, market cross, the stocks,
oak post with knee braces, grain sacks, bay window and shuttered window
(Style Pack 10 Medieval Village).
