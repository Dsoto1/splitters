Tile Trigger originals made with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai)

These models are the owner's own: generated on the owner's paid Meshy
subscription, which grants the subscriber ownership of the generated assets
and the right to use them commercially. They are original to this game (not
taken from any pack) and ship as part of it.

The owner's rule (2026-11-06): Meshy models from the owner's Drive are Meshy
Community CC0 unless the owner says they made them. Only models the owner says
they made, or that we generate through the Meshy API with the owner's
approval, are owned. The provenance audit of 2026-11-06 kept these, each for
the reason given; everything else that was recorded here came from the Drive
and is now listed in assets-src/meshy-community/License.txt and
assets-src/meshy-community-characters/License.txt (HANDOFF.md, "Intake of
2026-11-06", has the full list for the owner to confirm).

Each character's download file name (or Meshy task IDs) is recorded in
content/meshy.lock.json, the props' in content/props.lock.json and the
