Tile Trigger originals made with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai)

These models are the owner's own: generated on the owner's paid Meshy
subscription, which grants the subscriber ownership of the generated assets
and the right to use them commercially. They are original to this game (not
taken from any pack) and ship as part of it.

The owner's rule (2026-11-06): Meshy models from the owner's Drive are Meshy
Community CC0 unless the owner says they made them. Only models the owner says
they made, or that we generate through the Meshy API with the owner's
approval, are owned. The provenance audit of 2026-11-06 kept four groups here,
each for a reason on record; the owner then said of three of them, the same day:
"I did not make any, they are Meshy community." The airliner shell, the buggy
and its tire, and the Alien Biker are listed in
assets-src/meshy-community/License.txt and
assets-src/meshy-community-characters/License.txt now. What is owned:

- The mining machines (props mining_excavator and mining_haul_truck, pack
  meshy-owned-mining, for Iron Maw): generated on 2026-10-30 with Meshy's
  text-to-3D through our Meshy API on the owner's subscription, at the owner's
  request (preview tasks 01a18e38-f0e4-7577-8f3c-ea46f6cdd3a2 and
  01a18e38-f9bb-722c-9547-d9ad2cbce10e, with their refine tasks), built by
  tools/content/prepare-props.mjs from the textured GLB downloads. We made
  them through the API with the owner's approval, so they were never the
  owner's own downloads: they stay owned.
- Cool Hands Rex (character meshy_trex, pack meshy-owned): the download
  "Cool TRex 10k" (2026-11-06, rigged and animated on Meshy, from the owner's
  Drive; import list batch 1, docs/IMPORT_LIST.md). The owner, 2026-11-06:

  tools/content/prepare-guns.mjs.
- The walkable-deck excavator (prop mining_excavator_deck, pack
