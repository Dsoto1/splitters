Tile Trigger originals made with Meshy (https://www.meshy.ai)

These models were generated by the Tile Trigger owner on their own paid Meshy
subscription, which grants the subscriber ownership of the generated assets
and the right to use them commercially. They are original to this game (not
taken from any pack) and ship as part of it.

Each model's Meshy task IDs (preview, texture, rig), or for the textured FBX
downloads the download's file name, are recorded in content/meshy.lock.json;
tools/content/prepare-meshy-characters.mjs turns the downloads into the
game's character files (rigging the unrigged FBX ones itself). The rock pile
prop is built by tools/content/prepare-props.mjs (content/props.json, pack
meshy-owned-rock-pile). The raw downloads (FBX, PNG) are not kept in the repo.

Provenance: Meshy, owned (generated by the owner on their Meshy subscription).

The helmets worn as gear (content/gear.json) are built by
tools/content/prepare-gear.mjs from the owner's textured GLB downloads; each
download's file name is recorded in content/gear.lock.json.

The mining machines (props mining_excavator and mining_haul_truck, for Iron Maw) were generated on
2026-10-30 with Meshy's text-to-3D on the owner's subscription, at the owner's request, and built by
tools/content/prepare-props.mjs (pack meshy-owned-mining) from the textured GLB downloads.

The crates and cargo props (cargo_*: army, wooden, stacked and sci-fi crates, six shipping containers, a
gantry crane and an IBC tank) were generated on 2026-10-29/30 on the owner's subscription and built by
tools/content/prepare-props.mjs (pack meshy-owned-cargo) from the textured GLB downloads.
