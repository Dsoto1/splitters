What we use: the GLB versions (textures embedded: the 512 px hand-painted trim
sheet and the props' own maps), each re-encoded as a 256 px JPEG.

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-props.mjs
builds the game-ready sd_* props from the extracted download (the folder
"modular dungeon kit"; see docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and
content/props.lock.json records what it wrote.
