Quaternius releases his packs under CC0; the owner supplied this download as
CC0. The download itself (December 2017: Blends/, FBX/Zombie.fbx and
ZombieSmooth.fbx, OBJ/, Preview.mp4) carries no licence file.

What we use: FBX/Zombie.fbx (rigged: 48-bone humanoid skeleton, 2,116
triangles, clips ZombieIdle, ZombieWalk, ZombieRun, ZombieBite, ZombieCrawl)
and its palette Blends/ZombieTexture.png (32 x 32 px).

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository.
tools/content/prepare-creatures.mjs builds packages/client/public/creatures/zombie.glb
from the extracted download (see docs/CHARACTERS.md, "Creatures"), and
content/creatures.lock.json records what it wrote.
