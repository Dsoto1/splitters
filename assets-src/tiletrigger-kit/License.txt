Tile Trigger kit pieces (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, rounded bags, rings) by
tools/content/build-kit-props.mjs, which writes the GLB files in this folder.
Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same code writes the same files.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props.json and the provenance ledger).

Pieces: sandbag walls, corner and nest, gabion blast wall, steel hedgehog,
concrete tank trap, ammo crates, fuel drum, razor wire and a camouflage net
(Style Pack 01 Military Compound); mine cart and ore cart, dynamite crate and
bundle, hitching post, water trough, wagon wheel, wanted board, outhouse,
buffer stop, mine timber frame, tumbleweed, covered wagon and wind pump
(Style Pack 02 Frontier Dust).
