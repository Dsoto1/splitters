The set ships a .blend, FBX (Unity and Unreal) and one glTF binary holding
every piece; we read the glTF and attach the set's 1k colour maps (which the
glTF leaves unlinked), shrunk to 512 px JPEGs.

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-props.mjs
builds the game-ready props from the extracted download (see
docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and content/props.lock.json records what it wrote.
