Style Pack pieces: 03 Imperial Arena and 09 Sands of Egypt (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres, rings and flat sheets) by
tools/content/prepare-ancient.mjs with tools/content/pieces.mjs, which write
the GLB files in this folder. Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same
code writes the same files.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props.json and the provenance ledger).

Pieces: Ionic column, bronze brazier, emperor statue, amphorae, gladius rack,
training post, eagle standard, legion banner, racing chariot, beast cage,
stake barricade, marble altar, bench and bust, forum fountain, velarium
awning and the emperor's throne (Style Pack 03 Imperial Arena); obelisk,
sphinx, seated pharaoh, Anubis shrine, sarcophagus, canopic jars, papyrus
column, felucca, reed boat, date palm, papyrus reeds, fire stand, gilded
chest, stele and Bastet statue (Style Pack 09 Sands of Egypt).
