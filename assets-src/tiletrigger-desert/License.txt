Style Pack pieces: 09 Desert City (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres, rings and flat sheets) by
tools/content/prepare-desert.mjs with tools/content/pieces.mjs, which write
the GLB files in this folder. Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same
code writes the same files.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props.json and the provenance ledger).

Pieces: market stall, market canopy, banner, hanging lantern, palm planter,
water well, vase, jars, rug, timber crane, cloth line, scaffold, awning
window, lattice (mashrabiya) window, shuttered window, spice baskets, floor
cushions, rug rack, column and ornate arch (Style Pack 09 Desert City).
