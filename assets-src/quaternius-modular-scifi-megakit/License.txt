Raw pack files are not kept in this repository (the download is ~65 MB).
tools/content/prepare-props.mjs builds the game-ready props in
packages/client/public/props from the extracted download (see
docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md, "Pack imports from a downloads directory"), and
content/props.lock.json records what it wrote.
