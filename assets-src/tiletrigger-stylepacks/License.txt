Tile Trigger originals: Style Pack pieces modelled in code

These pieces (props such as the reactor core, viewport, force-field emitter,
cryo pod, vending machine and rooftop units, and the facade pieces of the
Station hull, Glass tower and Neon block facades) are made by the Tile Trigger
team from simple solids (boxes, cylinders, rings, spheres) written as code in
tools/content/prepare-packs.mjs with the modeller in tools/content/pieces.mjs.
No asset pack, scan, generated model or third-party file is used.

They are owned by the game and ship as part of it. Re-run
`node tools/content/prepare-packs.mjs` to rebuild them exactly.
