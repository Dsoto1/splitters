The page describes the pack as free low poly vehicles "for you to use as you
would like". Blender 2.9x / 3.0 FBX exports with one shared palette texture.

Not used: "docLorean" (a homage to a film's time-machine car).

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-props.mjs
builds the game-ready props in packages/client/public/props from the
extracted downloads (see docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and content/props.lock.json
records what it wrote.
