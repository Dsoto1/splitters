Tile Trigger originals: Style Pack props modelled in code

These props (Style Packs 05 Gothic Estate, 06 Jungle Ruins and 08 Suburban
Chaos: bookcases, chandelier, candelabra, clock, piano, armour, bed, desk,
sarcophagus, angel, headstone, railings and gates, hedges and topiary, gas
lamp; temple idols, pillars, stele, altar, vines, liana, rope-bridge anchor,
waterfall, roots, jungle plants, bamboo, brazier, golden idol; appliances,
barbecue, propane tank, mailbox, bins, lawnmower, gnome, picket fence,
playground pieces, petrol pump, family car, patio furniture and hydrant) are
made by the Tile Trigger team from simple solids (boxes, cylinders, rings,
spheres and turned slabs) written as code in tools/content/build-style-props.mjs
with the modeller in tools/content/pieces.mjs. No asset pack, scan, generated
model, AI service or third-party file is used.

They are owned by the game and ship as part of it (licence "Owned" in
content/props.json and the provenance ledger). Re-run
`node tools/content/build-style-props.mjs` to rebuild them exactly.
