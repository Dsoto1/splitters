Tile Trigger originals: the procedural environment, generated in code

The environment species (trees, conifers, shrubs, grass, boulders, cliff faces), their levels of detail and
far-away cards, and the shared bark, rock and leaf pictures are generated by the Tile Trigger team's own code,
tools/content/environment.mjs and tools/content/env/*.mjs, from the seeds and settings in
content/environment.json and content/environment/*.json. The code uses seeded noise only: no photo, scan,
asset pack, AI service or third-party file goes into them.

They are owned by the game and ship as part of it. Re-run `node tools/content/environment.mjs` to rebuild them
byte for byte.
