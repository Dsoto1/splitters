Gas station (PSX-style set)
Models by Elbolillo (Elbolilloduro) - https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/
Source page: https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/gas-station
(also on Sketchfab: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/gas-station-eeb913b90b4344ddbd7852f82a7ef160)

License: the itch.io page states "The models in this package are under the
CC0 license" (Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The Sketchfab copy is
listed as CC Attribution, so we credit "Elbolillo" either way.

Textures: the page says some textures were taken from pexels.com, and the
pack's textures are photo-based (branded goods among them). Their terms are
not the model licence, so we ship none of them: tools/content/flatten.mjs
replaces every texture with one flat colour per triangle and only the
geometry ships. The "6twelve" shop sign and logo are not used.

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-props.mjs
builds the game-ready props from the extracted download (see
docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and content/props.lock.json records what it wrote.
