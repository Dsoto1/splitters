Free CC0 Guns & Explosives Pack (Guns&Explosives.rar: 19 firearms and explosives)
Models by 3DModelsCC0 - https://3dmodelscc0.itch.io/
Source page: https://3dmodelscc0.itch.io/free-cc0-guns-explosives-pack

License (as stated on the source page and its tags): Creative Commons Zero
v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The models were first hosted on the author's own site (3DModelsCC0, now
closed); the author confirms in the page's comments that they modelled every
asset they share. The download holds Cinema 4D FBX exports with separate
Substance-style PBR maps and no licence file.

Used (2026-11-07, the owner's weapons intake of 2026-11-06: "We can replace our
tommy gun with the 3DModelsCC0 one, and bring in those others to replace any
one-off originals we have"):
- AT_MINE  -> guns/at_mine.glb (the thrown mine's model, since 2026-10-26)
- Suomi_KP  -> guns/suomi_kp.glb (the Submachine Gun)
- AK-47  -> guns/ak47_cc0.glb (the Assault Rifle)
- M4A1  -> guns/m4a1.glb (the Burst Rifle)
- Pistol_MK (Makarov)  -> guns/makarov.glb (the Pistol; its magazine, laid out
  beside the gun in the download, put back in the grip)
- GreaseGun  -> guns/grease_gun.glb (the Machine Pistol)
- Shotgun  -> guns/shotgun_cc0.glb (the Shotgun)
- Sniper  -> guns/sniper_cc0.glb
- Smoke_Grenade  -> guns/smoke_m18.glb (the Smoke Grenade)
- FragGrenade  -> guns/frag_m67.glb (the frag's look in the Military weapon set)
- Molotv_Cocktail  -> guns/molotov.glb (the Incendiary; its liquid a flat
  colour: Liquid_Base_Color.png does not unpack from the rar)
The guns ship three copies each (everyone's, and sharper ones for the gun in
your own hands at Medium and High graphics), all with the pack's own colour,
normal, metal and roughness maps (DirectX normal maps' green flipped for glTF).
Not used: the nuclear bomb and the flare gun (the owner: "not sure we need the
nuclear bomb or flare gun"), the flashbang (dropped by the owner over seizure
risk; its FBX doesn't unpack from the rar either), the Luger, C4, claymore,
M24 grenade and pipe bomb (spare: docs/IMPORT_LIST.md).

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-guns.mjs
builds the game-ready model from the extracted download (see
docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and content/guns.lock.json records what it wrote.
