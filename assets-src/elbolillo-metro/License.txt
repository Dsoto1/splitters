Metro (Metro_PSX, PSX-style subway set)
Models by Elbolillo (Elbolilloduro) - https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/
Source page: https://elbolilloduro.itch.io/metro
(also on Sketchfab: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/metro-b2017b6b23844e79a72b473b516a577e)

License: the itch.io page states "The models in this package are under the
CC0 license" (Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The Sketchfab copy is
listed as CC Attribution, so we credit "Elbolillo" either way.

Textures: the page credits some textures to ambientCG, TextureLib and
Textures.com, and several are photos (a vending machine with branded snacks,
posters). Textures.com's terms do not allow passing its textures on, so we
ship none of the pack's textures: tools/content/flatten.mjs replaces every
texture with one flat colour per triangle and only the geometry ships. The
posters and the metro map are not used.

Raw pack files are not kept in this repository. tools/content/prepare-props.mjs
builds the game-ready props from the extracted download (see
docs/CONTENT_PIPELINE.md), and content/props.lock.json records what it wrote.
