Meshy community models (https://www.meshy.ai)

Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), per the owner.

The owner downloaded these models from Meshy's community gallery and states
they are published there under CC0. The community page URL for each model is
pending from the owner; until it arrives the licence rests on the owner's
statement (recorded here and in docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md as "Meshy community,
CC0 (per owner)"). The community characters are listed in
assets-src/meshy-community-characters/License.txt.

Models (name as on the download, download file date):

Weapons and turrets (pack meshy-community-guns in content/guns.json, built by
tools/content/prepare-guns.mjs):

- "ANACONDA 44" (2026-01-26)  -> guns/ironclad_revolver.glb, the Heavy
  Revolver's model. The download is named after a real revolver product and
  its texture carried lettering (a garbled version of that name, a serial and
  a warning line), a skull decal with "NO MERCY" and orange tally marks on the
  grip: all painted out of the colour map at build time (`paintOut` in
  content/guns.json). The name is used nowhere in the game; the asset is
  called "Ironclad revolver" there.
- "Plasma Annihilator X" (2026-01-18)  -> guns/plasma_spin_gun.glb, the Spin
  Gun's model (a rotary-barrel gun).
- "Nebula Arc Cannon" (2026-07-03)  -> guns/arc_cannon_turret.glb, the Rocket
  Turret's model (a cannon on a pedestal, so not a hand-held weapon).

Props (pack meshy-community-props in content/props.json, built by
tools/content/prepare-props.mjs):

- "Industrial Control Panel" (2026-03-24)  -> prop mp_control_cabinet
  (Control Cabinet, Industrial)
- "Wreckage" (2025-12-21)  -> prop mp_burnt_car (Burnt-Out Car, Vehicles). No
  badge, plate or maker's mark on it.
- "Spiky Dinosaur" (2025-12-29)  -> prop mp_dino_statue (Dinosaur Statue,
  Jungle Ruins), its colours turned to stone. A generic carnotaur-like
  theropod: a biped, but with a level body, a tail as long again and tiny
  arms, so it doesn't fit the humanoid rig the NPCs share.

tools/content/meshy-mesh.mjs turns the textured FBX downloads into the shipped
files. The raw downloads (FBX, PNG) are not kept in the repo;
content/guns.lock.json and content/props.lock.json check the built files.
