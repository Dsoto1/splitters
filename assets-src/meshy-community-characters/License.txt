Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), per the owner.

The owner downloaded these models from Meshy's community gallery and states
they are published there under CC0. The community page URL for each model is
pending from the owner; until it arrives the licence rests on the owner's
statement (recorded here and in docs/ASSET_SOURCES.md as "Meshy community,
CC0 (per owner)").

Looked at and not used (they closely resemble real people, so they fail the
likeness screen):

- "T Pose Hero" (2026-05-19): a caricature of a recognisable musician's look,
  with a portrait of a real person printed on the shirt
- "T Pose Model" (2025-11-05): a photo-real likeness of a real person
- "T Pose Portrait" (2026-07-27): a photo-real likeness of a real person,
  made from a portrait photograph

tools/content/prepare-meshy-characters.mjs (FBX_CHARACTERS) rigs the textured
FBX downloads onto the game's skeleton and builds the shipped character files.
The raw downloads (FBX, PNG) are not kept in the repo; content/meshy.lock.json
checks the built files.
