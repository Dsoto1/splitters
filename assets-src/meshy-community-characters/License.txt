tools/content/prepare-meshy-characters.mjs (one manifest each in content/characters) rigs
the textured downloads onto the game's skeleton and builds the shipped character files.
The raw downloads (FBX, PNG) are not kept in the repo; content/meshy.lock.json
checks the built files.
