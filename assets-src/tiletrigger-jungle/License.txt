Style Pack pieces: 06 Jungle Ruins refresh (built in code)

These models are original to Tile Trigger. They are not taken from any pack
and no generative AI service made them: each one is assembled from plain
geometry (boxes, cylinders, cones, spheres, rings and flat sheets) by
tools/content/prepare-jungle.mjs with tools/content/pieces.mjs, which write
the GLB files in this folder. Re-run that script to rebuild them; the same
code writes the same files.

They belong to the Tile Trigger owner and ship as part of the game
(licence "Owned" in content/props.json and the provenance ledger).

Pieces: zipline post, crane and pulley, wooden platform, temple guardians
(large and small), stone obelisk, temple spire, fire bowl, hanging brazier,
wall torch, teal and red temple banners, tree root arch, mossy tree trunk,
jungle plant cluster, stone planter, temple pottery, rubble pile, collapsed
column, broken column, stone sarcophagus, spike row, chain rail, rope rail,
temple altar and overgrown beam (Style Pack 06 Jungle Ruins, refreshed from
the owner's Tileset 04 Jungle Ruins and Tileset 07 Ancient Temple sheets).
